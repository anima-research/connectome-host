- A status channel's notice names the alert's kind and says what happened in a fixed line for that kind ("context-refusal: its context couldn't be compiled for a request"), where it carried the alert's own message, up to 500 characters. A clear names the kind that cleared and what stays open, with no cause. The alert's message, which can carry a provider's error text, stays on the TUI's alert line, the Web UI, failures.log and the webhook, so neither a status channel's readers nor the agent's `[host-notice]` marker take it in.
