- A headless host sends `inference:round_text` with the text of each round that `inference:speech` doesn't carry, once the round ends: a round that ends in tool calls, a round of a silent start, and every round of an agent other than the main one, such as a subagent's. A round that fails, is aborted or is exhausted sends none, and so does a round with no text. A subscription takes it by name or by `*`. The fleet doesn't force it.
