- `identity--request` sends `PATCH`. Its method list, in both the advertised schema and the handler's check, stopped at GET/POST/PUT/DELETE, so a resident's PATCH was refused before it reached the service, and PATCH-only routes such as orrery's node update (starring, archiving, a curator's note) were out of reach. A PATCH carries a JSON `body` or a `fromFile` upload exactly as POST and PUT do. The service allowlist with host-attached access stays the boundary, and each service still authorizes the request. The `body` description and the `fromFile` refusal now say what the handler always did: a body goes with any method other than GET.
