- `llm-calls` error records from the generic logging wrapper (the path every non-Anthropic provider takes) bound their error text to 4,000 characters. The text is cut to its head and tail around a marker stating how many of how many characters were omitted, and no surrogate pair is split. A provider can echo the whole rejected request in its error body (one OpenAI-compatible 400 echoed ~1.7 MB), and the record already keeps that request as `rawRequest`. A record now also carries `errorType`, `httpStatus`, `providerErrorCode` and `retryable` when the error has them, plus `errorChars` when the text was cut. A small error is recorded exactly as before.
- An error whose fields cannot be read (a throwing getter) no longer makes the wrapper throw the getter's error in place of the provider's. It is logged as `[error could not be read]` and the original error is rethrown.
