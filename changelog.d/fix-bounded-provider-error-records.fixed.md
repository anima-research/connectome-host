- `llm-calls` error records from the generic logging wrapper bound their error text to 4,000 characters. That wrapper serves the `openai-responses`, `openai-compatible`, `openai-codex`, `openrouter` and `mock` providers; `anthropic` and `bedrock` log through their own classes, which this leaves unchanged. The text is cut to its head and tail around a marker stating how many of how many characters were omitted, and no surrogate pair is split. A provider can echo the whole rejected request in its error body (one OpenAI-compatible 400 echoed ~1.7 MB), and the record already keeps that request as `rawRequest`. A record now also carries `errorType`, `httpStatus`, `providerErrorCode` and `retryable` when the error has them, plus `errorChars` when the text was cut; `errorType` and `providerErrorCode` are cut the same way at 128 characters. A small error's text is recorded byte for byte as before.
- An error whose fields cannot be read (a throwing getter) no longer makes the wrapper throw the getter's error in place of the provider's. Each field is read on its own: an unreadable `message` is recorded as `[message could not be read]` after the error's name, keeping the classification; an unreadable classification field is left out, keeping the text; and a value that cannot be read at all, such as a revoked Proxy, is recorded as `[error could not be read]`. The original error is always rethrown.
