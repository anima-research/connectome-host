- A subagent's failed run is retried by membrane's classification when agent-framework rejects it with membrane's error as the cause: a rate limit by its type or a 429, and a transient failure by a `timeout` or `network` type or a 500, 502, 503, 504 or 529. With such a cause, a 504 is retried though its words don't say "timeout". Where membrane couldn't type the failure from a status, as `unknown` or as `server` with no status, the provider's words decide, as before, and so do a failure's own words when it has no such cause.
