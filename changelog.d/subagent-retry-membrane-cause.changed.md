- A subagent's failed run is retried by membrane's classification when agent-framework rejects it with a copy of membrane's error as the cause, read by its name and fields: a rate limit by its type or a 429, and a transient failure by a `timeout` or `network` type or a 500, 502, 503, 504 or 529. With such a cause, a 504 is retried though its words don't say "timeout". Elsewhere the words decide, as before: where membrane couldn't type the failure from a status, as `unknown` or as `server` with no status, and where the failure has no membrane cause. The words read are the error's own and its cause's, so a failure that agent-framework words as "the turn failed" is retried as its original words were.
