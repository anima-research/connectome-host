- **Readline mode (`--no-tui` with stdin on a terminal):** A line typed while the agent's turn runs waits for the turn after it, and lines entered together, as a terminal sends a paste, are answered in order, each in a turn of its own, a command among them running as it's read, unless a turn runs past their limits: lines that go in while a turn runs are answered together, in the turn after it; a line still waiting to go in when the person asks for a session switch isn't sent, and neither is one holding what the person then entered hidden, and the host says so of each, naming the line's start unless it holds what was entered hidden, which the host says without quoting it; and when the conversation ends the host says how many lines hadn't gone in; and the prompt is drawn once nothing the person sent is still waiting for its answer, so each answer prints before the prompt that follows it, unless a turn runs past 120 s, when the host says it goes on, and that the answer prints when it comes; before, the prompt could come before an answer and none after it. A line's 120 s limit counts from when nothing sent before it, setup's opening included, is still waited on, and not while setup asks for a secret, and an answer that comes after its line's limit, or one to no line, such as a wake's, is followed by the prompt again. Setup's opening has no limit of its own, so a line typed while it runs waits until it ends. A line whose handling fails is said to have failed, with why, and the host goes on with the prompt; before, it ended readline mode.
- **Readline mode's notice of where the host's diagnostics go** reads "The host's diagnostics go to PATH, not to this terminal.", where it read "Error log: PATH".
- **Batch mode** (stdin not on a terminal, without `--headless`) waits for each line's turn the same way: a line sent while a turn runs waits for the turn after it, or its 120 s limit, where before its wait could end with the turn that was running. A turn that an event waiting at the host's boot begins counts too, and batch and readline modes print it from its start, where before they printed only what it streamed after they began following the host.
