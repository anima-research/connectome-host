- A release switch no longer cuts a fleet child's turn that started after the child answered it. The switch's last look now quiesces nothing: a child quiesced, for the switch or by its operator, holds on it, and until the switch is decided refuses any resume of it, from the Web UI, `/update restart resume` or an MCPL server's host command, and what would change its session, `/session new` and `/session switch` among it, since a new session's framework serves at once. A child that isn't quiesced at the last look keeps that try from going ahead, so a Resume on a child during the switch's wait now stops the try, where the last look quiesced the child again; a child started during the wait likewise holds the try back, and the next try quiesces it. Stop now looks again before it stops, and is called off where a child's person resumed that child, or where this host's own quiesce was ended during the wait, where it stopped and cut that turn. A child the fleet starts, starts again or stops during the last look calls the switch off.
