- **Web UI health:** a compression quarantine that a resident's strategy marks `unreadable` no longer clears a standing quarantine alert. `/healthz` now treats such a status as a failed read: the agent has no entry in `compressionQuarantine`, and `readFailures` names it, so the alert stays until a read succeeds, and the Health tab says the quarantine couldn't be read. A reader of `/healthz` that looked for `unreadable` in `compressionQuarantine` reads `readFailures` instead.
