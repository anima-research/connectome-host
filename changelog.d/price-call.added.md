- `src/call-pricing.ts` exports `priceCall`, a call's list price on `anthropic` or `openai-responses`. Anthropic's is `priceAnthropicCall`'s. OpenAI's comes from its pricing page as of November 9, 2026, at the tier the call reports (`default`, or none, as Standard; `flex` as Flex; `fast` or `priority` as Fast; `ultrafast` as Ultrafast), and at the long-context rates for a request over 272,000 input tokens. It gives no price on `openai-codex`, `openrouter`, `openai-compatible` or `bedrock`, nor for a tier, model or part of a call the page gives no rate for. The operator ledger and kv-unified's cache prices are unchanged.
