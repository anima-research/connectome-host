- Recipes can set `agent.strategy.imageCeiling` and `imageByteCeiling` for the autobiographical and frontdesk strategies, validated as the other image keys are, as non-negative safe integers. They're context-manager's image ceilings: the most top-level images, and the most of their base64 bytes, the live window keeps, whatever `maxLiveImages` and `maxLiveImageBytes` say, and the most a compression prompt carries. 0 means no ceiling.
