- Recipes can set `agent.strategy.imageCeiling` and `imageByteCeiling` for the autobiographical and frontdesk strategies, validated as the other image keys are, as non-negative safe integers, so a malformed value fails the recipe at load. They're context-manager's image ceilings: the most images, and the most of their base64 bytes, the live window keeps, whatever `maxLiveImages` and `maxLiveImageBytes` say, and the most a compression prompt carries. In the live window the limits and the ceilings count every image, whether top-level, nested in a tool result or generated, except one inside an opaque provider item. Within a turn, messages a tool boundary injects are rendered as the next compile will send them, the limits applied over their batch where the strategy can place them. The rest of what the turn adds, its tool results' images included, isn't held to the limits until the next compile. 0 means no ceiling. To keep no limit, set that ceiling to 0; to keep a limit above a ceiling, raise the ceiling to it. Raising `MEMBRANE_MAX_REQUEST_BYTES` doesn't raise `imageByteCeiling`; to keep more image bytes, raise the ceiling too, and set `maxLiveImageBytes` to 0 or at least as high.
